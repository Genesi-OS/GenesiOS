# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, moderators and maintainers pledge to make
participation in the Genesi OS community a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Where this applies

This Code applies to every space the project runs or represents:

- the repositories of the [Genesi-OS](https://github.com/Genesi-OS) organisation
  — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, code review and code;
- the community forum at [forum.genesios.org](https://forum.genesios.org);
- the documentation wiki and any project chat or social account;
- any space where you are officially representing the project, such as posting
  from an official account or acting as a maintainer at an event.

It applies to everyone equally — first-time reporters, long-standing
contributors, moderators and maintainers alike.

## Our standards

Behaviour that builds this community:

- showing empathy and kindness toward other people;
- being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- accepting responsibility, apologising to those affected by our mistakes, and
  learning from them;
- focusing on what is best for the community as a whole, not just for us;
- remembering that many people here are not writing in their first language, and
  reading generously.

Behaviour that is unacceptable:

- sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment, including sustained unwanted contact;
- publishing others' private information — a physical or email address, real
  name, workplace or account details — without their explicit permission;
- deliberately posting someone else's credentials, tokens or private files;
- dismissing or belittling someone for their level of experience;
- sharing malware, exploits aimed at project users, or deliberately harmful
  instructions dressed up as help;
- persistent off-topic disruption, spam, or advertising;
- encouraging or organising any of the above;
- other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

### A note specific to this project

Genesi OS documentation and support threads contain commands that modify or
erase systems. Deliberately giving someone a destructive command as a prank, or
knowingly withholding a warning that a command is destructive, is treated as a
serious violation of this Code, not as humour.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Maintainers and forum moderators are responsible for clarifying and enforcing
these standards, and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in
response to behaviour they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive or
harmful.

They have the right and responsibility to remove, edit or reject comments,
commits, code, wiki edits, issues, forum posts and other contributions that are
not aligned with this Code, and will communicate reasons for moderation
decisions when appropriate.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it to
<contact@genesios.org>. Put `CONDUCT` in the subject line.

You may also use the in-forum report control on any topic or reply, which routes
the content to moderators without making your report public.

Please include, as far as you are able:

- where it happened, with links or screenshots;
- what happened, in your own words;
- whether it is ongoing;
- anything you would like us to be careful about in how we respond.

**All reports are reviewed promptly and treated confidentially.** We will not
share your identity with the person reported without your agreement. Reporting
in good faith will never itself be held against you, even if we conclude no
violation occurred.

If your report concerns a maintainer, say so — it will be handled by someone
other than the person concerned wherever that is possible in a project of this
size. Where it is not possible, we will tell you so plainly rather than pretend
otherwise, so that you can decide how you wish to proceed.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers will follow these guidelines in determining the consequences of a
violation.

### 1. Correction

**Impact:** use of inappropriate language or other behaviour deemed
unprofessional or unwelcome.

**Consequence:** a private written warning, with clarity about the nature of the
violation and why the behaviour was inappropriate. A public apology may be
requested.

### 2. Warning

**Impact:** a violation through a single incident or series of actions.

**Consequence:** a warning with consequences for continued behaviour. No
interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with
those enforcing this Code, for a specified period. This includes avoiding
project spaces as well as external channels. Violating these terms may lead to a
temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary ban

**Impact:** a serious violation of community standards, including sustained
inappropriate behaviour.

**Consequence:** a temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public
communication with the community for a specified period. No public or private
interaction with the people involved is allowed during this period. Violating
these terms may lead to a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent ban

**Impact:** demonstrating a pattern of violation of community standards,
including sustained inappropriate behaviour, harassment of an individual, or
aggression toward or disparagement of classes of individuals.

**Consequence:** a permanent ban from any sort of public interaction within the
community.

Immediate action at any level may be taken without prior warning where content
is illegal, where there is a credible threat to someone's safety, or where an
account is being used for spam or abuse.

## Appeals

If you believe a moderation decision against you was mistaken or
disproportionate, you may appeal once by writing to <contact@genesios.org>
within 30 days, explaining why.

Appeals are reviewed by someone who was not solely responsible for the original
decision wherever that is practicable. We will tell you the outcome and the
reasoning. Bans on accounts used exclusively for spam, malware or illegal
content are not subject to appeal.

## Relationship to the Terms of Service

This Code sets the behavioural standard for the community. The
[Terms of Service](https://www.genesios.org/terms) are the binding agreement
governing use of the project's services, and clause 12 of those Terms covers
moderation, suspension and termination. Where a behaviour is addressed by both,
they are read together; the Terms prevail on questions of legal effect.
